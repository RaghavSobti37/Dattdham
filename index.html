Dattadas Maharaj Ji

From service to a living Sankalp.

Years of Gau Seva and listening to gaushalas across India gave form to a single resolve: to nurture nine lakh Gau Matas with care, continuity and dignity.

Nine lakh lives. One enduring responsibility.

Instead of blaming darkness, why not light one lamp?

The GauGanga Sankalp

9,00,000

Gau Matas

One resolve. Nine lakh lives.

This is not a number alone. It is nine lakh individual lives entrusted to our care.

The Sankalp is to build a self-reliant current of life rooted in Sanatan values, where Gau Seva strengthens nature, families and society.
